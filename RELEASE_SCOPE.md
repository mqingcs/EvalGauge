# Release scope

## Included

- `src/`: EvalGauge compiler, parsers, matchers, unit algebra, evaluators, reports, and benchmark adapters.
- `scripts/`: deterministic audit, rescoring, analysis, quick verification, figure, review, and upstream-fetch entry points.
- `tests/`: the regression, unit, and property-based test suite.
- `data/`: all 90,871 generated case decisions, compiled contracts, censuses, model-score traces, and score-sensitivity tables used by the paper.
- `analysis/`: aggregate results and the exact machine-readable summaries underlying reported numbers.
- `artifacts/`: the browsable report, minimal failures, public-score transitions, two blinded 35-case review records, the three-case adjudication, the adjudicated casebook, paper tables, three licensed teaser crops, and compact ChartQAPro results.
- `frozen/`: only the source-version metadata, evaluator snapshots, licenses, audit policy, and unit registry needed for deterministic reconstruction.
- `paper/`: the submitted PDF, self-contained MMM 2027 LNCS source under `paper/mmm2027/`, three figures used by the paper, artifact statement, and claim-evidence ledger.

## Deliberately excluded

- Full MeasureBench and ChartQAPro image datasets.
- The external MeasureBench repository and its raw 4,884 prediction files; the public files can be fetched by immutable revision with `scripts/fetch_public_measurebench.py`, while their derived case-level scores are included.
- Provider API credentials, headers, account data, network logs, billing records, and raw call ledgers.
- Unlinked exploratory model outputs that do not enter a paper claim.
- Git history, local paths, editor state, caches, virtual environments, temporary files, and build intermediates.
- `artifacts/official_vs_typesafe.csv` and `artifacts/violations.jsonl`, which duplicate decisions already present in the included Parquet case tables and can be regenerated by the audit pipeline.
- Superseded figures and exploratory notes not used by the final paper.
- Superseded single-review derivatives; the distributed manual evidence is the two-reviewer record, seven-case SOP return, and third-reviewer adjudication.
- Redundant paper-figure copies outside the self-contained `paper/mmm2027/` source bundle.

This boundary preserves every reported result without publishing unrelated process material.
